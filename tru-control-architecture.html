How the whole system fits together

The heater talks to a Raspberry Pi over its LIN-UART service port. From there, the Tru-Control iPhone app can reach that gateway two different ways — directly over Bluetooth with no internet involved, or remotely through an MQTT broker you either self-host or rent from a cloud provider.

inetbox.py

Reads the heater's live status over LIN-UART. Everything else on the gateway builds on this.

truma_ble_server.py

Exposes that status over Bluetooth LE, for control with no router or internet in the loop.

mqtt_bridge_complete.py

Publishes it to whichever MQTT broker you've configured, for control from anywhere.

Either broker works with the same app and the same gateway — only where the data physically travels changes. A self-hosted broker keeps every byte on hardware you control, at no ongoing cost; a cloud broker trades that for a five-minute setup.
